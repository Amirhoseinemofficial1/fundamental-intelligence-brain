# Hypotheses and Knowledge

`FeatureInteractionHypothesisEngine` fits a chronological out-of-sample baseline and an explicit interaction model. It records every experiment and hypothesis. Only a finite interaction with at least 2% validation-MAE improvement becomes Knowledge; rejected hypotheses delete any stale corresponding knowledge record.

The daily research loop tests fundamental/source, gold/regime, derivatives and on-chain/liquidity combinations. Existing event/asset knowledge keeps sample, effect, uncertainty, temporal-stability and evidence level.
